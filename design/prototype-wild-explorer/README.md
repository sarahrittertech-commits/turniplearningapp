# Wild Explorer prototype (Oct 11–12, 2026)

A copy of the clickable iPhone prototype built on a Claude Design canvas, kept here for reference.

- **Live canvas:** <https://claude.ai/artifact/E4kw26v9GuywMnKsQR14mJ>. Private to Sarah's Claude account; open it while signed in, or share it from the canvas's Share menu. To keep editing it from another machine, give Claude the link.
- **These files** are the canvas's source: one `.dc.html` per screen, plus `canvas.json` for the layout. They won't render on their own: images point at the canvas's uploaded files (`/_blob/…`), and `support.js` comes from the canvas.

| File | Screen |
|---|---|
| `Main.dc.html` | Home: "Where to today?", keep watching, world cards, today's expedition |
| `World.dc.html` | Animals world: featured trail, topic photo grid, more trails |
| `Expedition.dc.html` | Ocean Friends expedition: 4 stops as a trail with stamps and a badge |
| `Backpack.dc.html` | Downloads ("Backpack"), with the grown-ups-only button |
| `Login.dc.html` | Grown-up sign-in. **Parked:** Sarah will redo branding and login. |
| `Player.dc.html` | Landscape player, paused state with Keep watching / Go home |
| `StyleGuide.dc.html` | Wild Explorer style tile. **Partly superseded** by the brand guide. |
| `Brand.dc.html` | Wordmark font comparison. **Superseded:** Fraunces chosen (brand guide §3). |

## Status

The prototype predates the brand guide. Still valid:
- the screen structure
- the line icons in colored circles
- the bold world colors on world cards
- photo cards
- expeditions

To be replaced when the prototype is updated:
- Barlow Condensed and all-caps titles → Fraunces / DM Sans
- the cream background (`#F6EFDF`) → a light background chosen in guide §13.9
- navy `#14213D` and dark purple → the brand palette

See [docs/brand/design-review-2026-10.md](../../docs/brand/design-review-2026-10.md) and [docs/brand/turnip-brand-guide.md](../../docs/brand/turnip-brand-guide.md).
